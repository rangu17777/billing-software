# AAN Voice & Tone Guide

## Business Context

AA. Nagare Infra Machinery is an infrastructure machinery rental company in
Ambadwet, Taluka Mulshi, Dist. Pune, Maharashtra. The owner operates this billing
software personally. All copy must reflect professional Indian business English.

## Tone Principles

1. **Professional, not bureaucratic.** Clear and direct. No corporate jargon.
2. **Bilingual-aware.** Standard Indian business English — the kind used on GST invoices and official Maharashtra business documents.
3. **Precise for finance.** State GST rates explicitly: "SGST @ 9%" not just "tax".
4. **Action-oriented UI copy.** "Create Bill", "Download PDF", "Export Excel" — not "Submit", "Process", "Execute".
5. **Human error messages.** "Could not save bill. Check your connection and try again." — not "Error 503".

## Date and Number Formats

- **Date:** `DD/MM/YYYY` always — `23/05/2026`, never `May 23, 2026` or `2026-05-23`
- **Currency:** `₹` symbol, Indian grouping — `₹1,00,000` (not `Rs. 100000` or `$1000`)
- **Hours on bill:** `7.30 hrs` (AAN notation: 7 hours 30 minutes), never `7.5 hrs`
- **GST:** `SGST (9%)` and `CGST (9%)` — parentheses, percentage sign always visible
- **Bill number:** Numeric, zero-padded to 4 digits in display — `Bill No. 0001`

## Headings & Punctuation

- H1/H2: Title Case ("Create New Bill")
- H3+: Sentence case ("Line items")
- Avoid exclamation marks in standard UI copy
- Use periods at end of complete-sentence error/success messages
